CINE GRADE

Colour grade your photos on the GPU, then share the look.

Your originals stay untouched

CineGrade is a desktop app for grading photos. Every adjustment runs on your graphics card through OpenGL and is stored as an edit, so you can undo it or start over at any time.

Before After

One path from import to export

Bring in a shoot, rate and label the keepers, and compare before and after with one shortcut. Copy an edit from one photo and paste it onto the next.

  1. ImportPick the photos for a project
  2. DevelopGrade, rate and compare
  3. ExportWrite the finished files

Take the look with you

Save a grade as a preset for your next project. Export finished photos as JPEG or TIFF, or turn the grade into a 3D LUT that other software can load.

A place to share your grades

The CineGrade community is on its way. You'll be able to post your edits and LUTs here, and download looks from other people straight into the app.
